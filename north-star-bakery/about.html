Our Story

Founded in 2006, North Star Bakery began with one oven, a borrowed yeast starter, and a commitment to high-quality, always-fresh baking. Over the past two decades, we have expanded from a modest neighborhood bakery into a high-volume artisanal bakery, serving the Atlanta area with a large variety of fresh loaves, pastries, and cookies.

Every recipe relies on locally sourced ingredients whenever possible, slow fermentation, and expert techniques that honor our committment to quality. Our products contain no artificial preservatives, allowing natural fermentation to develop the depth, texture, and flavor our customers demand.

Meet the Head Baker: Agnes Scout

Agnes Scout discovered her passion for baking as a little girl, helping her grandmother in the kitchen whenever she could. As an adult, Agnes began working through regional bakeries in the Southeast and constantly perfecting her craft. With over thirty years of hands-on baking expertise, Agnes serves as the guiding hand behind every recipe developed at North Star Bakery.

"Quality baking is patience manifest. When you work with simple ingredients, flour, water, salt, and yeast, you can expect delicious results that will please every customer."

In addition to supervising daily production, Agnes leads weekend community workshops teaching the chemistry of the baking process and tips and tricks to aspiring home bakers.

Our Values

  • Locally Sourced Ingredients: Locally sourced grains, stone-milled flours, eggs and dairy from local farms.
  • Quality Fermentation: 24-to-36-hour proofing cycles for optimal rise, flavor, and crust.
  • Zero Shortcuts: No dough conditioners, bleached additives, or commercial shortcuts.
  • Fresh Baked Daily: No pre-made, frozen doughs and breads like many grocery stores. We bake fresh daily.
